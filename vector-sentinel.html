VECTOR SENTINEL

You are the sentinel. A rogue AI is flooding the containment grid with hostile nodes. Steer, aim, and neutralize every threat wave before the grid falls.

MOVE WASD / Arrows thrust the ship in any direction.
AIM / FIRE Mouse aims. Click or Space fires bolts.
EMP Kills charge the EMP meter. When full, press E or Right-Click to unleash a shockwave that wipes the screen.
SCORE Destroy enemies for points. Chain kills to build a combo multiplier up to x3. Getting hit resets it.
THREATS Kill the rare EMP CARRIER to instantly refill your EMP. Watch for telegraphs - dashers and orbiters warn before they strike.
SURVIVE Higher waves bring new enemy types and tougher mixes. Shield regenerates when you stay clear.

SYSTEM OPERATIONAL MANUAL

  • ▶ Wave Composition: Each wave is a curated mix of enemy types. Every few waves introduces something new - learn what each threat does and prioritize targets.
  • ▶ Combo Multiplier: Chain kills without getting hit. The multiplier climbs toward x3, then resets if the grid touches you.
  • ▶ EMP Shockwave Discharge: Kills charge your EMP meter. A full meter lets you wipe the screen - but EMP Carriers refill it instantly.
  • ▶ Target Priority: Tanks and Orbiters are worth the most. Dashers and SPLITTERs announce their attacks - dodge the telegraph, then punish.
  • ▶ Procedural Synth Sound Engine: Integrated Web Audio synthesizer generates laser discharge, explosions, EMP sweeps, and threat warnings.
