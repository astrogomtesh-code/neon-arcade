How to Play & Controls

Cyberpunk Tetris is a high-speed matrix alignment survival system. Test your reaction times on the digital grid with these control systems:

  • Keyboard Controls: Use the A / ◀ or D / ▶ keys to shift left and right. Press ▲ or X to rotate, ▼ for soft drop, and SPACE for instant hard drop.
  • Touch Controls: Tap the interactive control pad buttons situated below the grid to shift, rotate, or drop active blocks.
  • Game Mechanics: Form unbroken horizontal rows across the 10x20 matrix grid. Clearing multiple lines simultaneously grants massive exponential score bonuses.

Strategy Guide & Tips

Surviving terminal velocity requires predicting piece transitions early and moving smoothly. Review these pro tips:

  • Keep Your Focus Ahead: Do not just look at your falling block. Watch the NEXT panel on the right where incoming blocks spawn so you can plan path transitions early.
  • Smooth Transits: Avoid panic double-shifting. Map out paths that require minimal switching to avoid being trapped on the outer boundaries.
  • Maintain Streak Multiplier: Utilize the HOLD feature (C or SHIFT) to save long pieces for quadruple-line clear 'Tetris' streaks to maximize your score output.
