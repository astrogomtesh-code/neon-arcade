PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

INTRODUCTION

At EUXODES, we respect your privacy. This policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and use.

COOKIES & TRACKING

EUXODES does not currently use cookies, show advertisements, or run third-party analytics or tracking scripts. No advertising or analytics partners collect or receive information about your use of this site.

LOCAL STORAGE

We use browser localStorage specifically to store your game high scores locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to our servers, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's data for this site.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

EUXODES does not currently load any third-party ad networks, analytics tools, or tracking services. The site is hosted by Vercel, which delivers the pages you request, but the site itself does not collect any visitor data.
