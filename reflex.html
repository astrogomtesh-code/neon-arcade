REFLEX

How to Play & Controls

Reflex is a lane-switching reaction game. Red barrier blocks fall from the top of the screen and the game keeps getting faster, so you have to read each block and move to the right lane in time.

  • Keyboard Controls: Use the A or Left Arrow key to move one lane left. Use the D or Right Arrow key to move one lane right.
  • Touch Controls: Tap the left side of the game screen to shift left, or the right side of the game screen to shift right.
  • Game Mechanics: Dodge oncoming red barrier blocks. Passing a block in the lane right next to yours builds your dodge streak, which raises your score multiplier. If a block passes two or more lanes away, the streak resets.

Strategy Guide & Tips

Surviving terminal velocity requires predicting lane layout changes and moving smoothly. Review these pro tips:

  • Keep Your Focus Ahead: Do not just look at your own character. Watch the top of the lanes where new obstacles spawn so you can plan your path transitions early.
  • Smooth Transits: Avoid double shifting lanes in a panic. Map out paths that require minimal switching to avoid being trapped on the outer boundaries.
  • Build Your Streak: Passing a block in the lane right next to yours increases your streak multiplier. Move only when you need to - a block that passes two or more lanes away resets your streak.

The Reflex Protocol (Lore)

The virtual grid is shifting, the lanes are narrowing, and your reflexes are the only thing keeping your program online. In the virtual space of the Reflex Protocol, runners are thrust into a high-octane five-lane terminal path. As you accelerate, the central firewall spawns purging block patterns in unpredictable arrays, demanding instant transitions.

The engine constantly accelerates, pushing your nervous system to the absolute limit. Will you conquer the terminal velocity, or will you end up as another fragmented packet in the digital abyss? Stay alert, switch fast, and survive!
