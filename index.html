Seven Browser Arcade Games

EUXODES is a small collection of browser games built with HTML5 Canvas. There is nothing to download and no account to create - each game opens in your browser and saves your high score locally on your device.

About the Games

Cyber Snake is a snake survival game where you steer through the grid, harvest data nodes, and dodge firewall mines. Cyberpunk Tetris is a block-stacking game where you clear rows while the fall speed keeps increasing. Comet Belt has you flying a comet across five lanes, dodging waves of asteroids. Void Dash is an auto-runner where you jump and dash through obstacles. Reflex tests your reaction time as you dodge falling blocks across five lanes. Vector Sentinel is a 360° vector survival shooter where you neutralize enemy nodes and unleash EMP shockwaves. Neon Breakout is a neon paddle-and-ball breaker where you breach the firewall one block at a time.

All seven games get faster as you survive, and your best score for each game is stored in your browser.
