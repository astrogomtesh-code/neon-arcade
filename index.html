Two Modes, Eight Games

EUXODES is a small collection of browser games built with HTML5 Canvas. There is nothing to download and no account to create - each game opens in your browser and saves your high score locally on your device.

CLASSIC brings together traditional arcade gameplay: Cyberpunk Tetris, Neon Breakout, Void Dash, and Neon Tomb. RUSH is the fast-paced action and survival lineup: Reflex, Comet Belt, Vector Sentinel, and Cyber Snake.

All eight games get faster as you survive, and your best score for each game is stored in your browser.
