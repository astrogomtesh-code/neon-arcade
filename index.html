Five Browser Arcade Games

Neon Arcade is a small collection of browser games built with HTML5 Canvas. There is nothing to download and no account to create - each game opens in your browser and saves your high score locally on your device.

About the Games

Cyber Snake is a snake survival game where you steer through the grid, harvest data nodes, and dodge firewall mines. Cyberpunk Tetris is a block-stacking game where you clear rows while the fall speed keeps increasing. Comet Belt has you flying a comet across five lanes, dodging waves of asteroids. Void Dash is an auto-runner where you jump and dash through obstacles. Reflex tests your reaction time as you dodge falling blocks across five lanes.

All five games get faster as you survive, and your best score for each game is stored in your browser.
