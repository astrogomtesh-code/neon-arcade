COMET BELT

How to Play & Controls

In Comet Belt, your comet flies forward across five vertical lanes. Each wave of asteroids blocks some of the lanes, so you move up or down into one of the lanes that stays open. Hitting any asteroid ends the run.

  • Keyboard Controls: Press the W or Up Arrow key to move up a lane. Press the S or Down Arrow key to move down a lane.
  • Touch/Mobile Controls: Tap the upper half of the screen to move up, or the lower half of the screen to move down.
  • Objective: Steer your comet through the gaps between oncoming asteroids. Colliding with any asteroid instantly ends the run.

Strategy Guide & Tips

Surviving the comet storm requires anticipating obstacles and positioning your comet perfectly. Read these flight tips to improve your high score:

  • Fly Close for Near-Miss Streaks: You earn 10 points for every wave you survive. A near miss - changing lanes just before an asteroid passes your position, or slipping past one in an adjacent lane - pays 100 points multiplied by your streak. The streak resets after about 4 seconds without another near miss.
  • Stay Positioned near the Center: Return to the center lane when possible. This gives you the maximum time and space to react to newly spawned asteroids from either the top or bottom boundaries.
  • Predict Spawn Trajectories: Asteroids move at varying speeds. Watch the right side of the screen carefully to analyze which ones are moving fastest and plan your lane changes in advance.

The Comet Belt Chronicles (Lore)

Beneath the glowing neon rings of the outer rim lies the Comet Belt—a dense, chaotic storm of hyper-velocity space debris and heavy radioactive elements. As a rogue runner piloting your comet across this forbidden sector, you are carrying classified data packets.

The stellar wind is wild, and the asteroids are fast. With each asteroid you narrowly dodge, your comet's battery charges up, reinforcing your defense signal. But make one wrong turn, and the kinetic impact will vaporize your comet into stardust. Keep your eyes sharp, stay clear of the asteroids, and conquer the belt!
