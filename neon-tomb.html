Lore: The Infinite Cyber-Crypt

Deep within the subterranean layers of the Neon Arcade network lies the Neon Tomb—an unmapped digital labyrinth designed to trap rogue AI entities. As an escaped vector probe, your core operating system moves in strict grid steps: each input advances you exactly one cell. Ascend infinitely upward to escape the ascending data shredder before your code is purged.

Controls & Movement Mechanics

  • • Desktop Controls: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move one grid cell at a time (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • • Mobile Controls: Swipe firmly across the screen in any cardinal direction to move one cell.
  • • Grid Movement: Each press advances exactly one cell. If the next cell is a solid wall, you stay in place - you never slide past an opening.

Hazards & Hostile Entities

  • • Firewall Shredder: A lethal rising plasma wall climbing from the bottom of the tomb. Halting for too long guarantees deletion.
  • • Laser Gates: High-voltage optical barriers that cycle on and off periodically across corridor intersections.
  • • Spike Pits: Concealed hazard plates that deploy fatal energy spikes when trodden on.
  • • Hunter Drones: Aggressive security units that spawn in unvisited crypt sectors and hunt down your signal along grid axes when within range.

Survival & Escalation Strategies

Every 40 meters ascended, the tomb escalates security parameters: the firewall shredder accelerates, enemy drone reaction times decrease, and trap density tightens. Follow the glowing cyan EXIT portal at the top of each sector and step into it to clear that sector and keep climbing.
