How to Play & Controls

Cyber Snake is a high-velocity 360° directional survival system. Navigate the digital grid, collect energy nodes, and evolve your neon trail without severing your core program:

  • Keyboard Steering: Press A / ◀ to rotate left and D / ▶ to rotate right. Hold W or SHIFT for Overdrive Turbo, and press SPACE to trigger an EMP shockwave when charged.
  • Mouse & Touch Steering: Move your cursor or drag on the game canvas to steer directly toward your finger/pointer, or tap the interactive touch pad beneath the screen.
  • Power-Ups & Core Upgrades: Absorb Cyan Data Nodes to grow, collect Yellow Overdrive Orbs for speed multipliers, and grab Magenta Shield Cores to withstand lethal collisions.

Strategy Guide & Tips

Mastering the digital grid requires fluid arc movement, rapid power-up acquisition, and perimeter management:

  • Build Your Combo Multiplier: Ingest energy nodes in rapid succession before the temporal combo bar depletes to stack score multipliers up to 5X.
  • Coil & Loop Tactics: Use smooth 360-degree turning to loop back into open space. Avoid boxing yourself into tight corners as your glowing tail trail lengthens.
  • Deploy EMP Blast Wisely: Reserve your EMP shockwave charge (SPACE) for emergency escapes when trapped near moving firewall mines or your own tail.

The Cyber Snake Protocol (Lore)

Deep within the mainframe, autonomous data-harvesting vectors known as Cyber Snakes navigate endless streams of light memory. As security protocols intensify, corrupted firewall hazards drift into the sector to purge non-registered programs.

Equipped with custom steering thrusters, EMP shock emitters, and shield modulators, your mission is to absorb high-density data payloads without collapsing into your own lingering energy tail. Accelerate, overcome the system firewalls, and achieve digital transcendence!
