VOID DASH

How to Play & Controls

Void Dash is an auto-runner: the game moves your character forward on its own, and you only control jumping and dashing. Survive as long as you can - one hit ends the run.

  • Keyboard Controls: Use the Space, W or Up Arrow to jump. Press Shift, D or Right Arrow to dash forward when your dash meter is full.
  • Touch Controls: Tap the left side of the screen to jump, or the right side to dash.
  • Double Jumping: Press jump again in mid-air to perform a double jump and clear taller obstacles.
  • Dashing: Dashing grants temporary invincibility and allows you to shatter incoming obstacles for bonus points.
  • Obstacles: Grid barriers and asteroids can be jumped or dashed through. Sentinel drones float at different heights, so a single jump is not always enough. Spire pillars are too tall to jump, so dash straight through them.

Strategy Guide & Tips

Survival in the grid requires more than just raw speed. Review these advanced strategies to achieve your best possible scores:

  • Precision Dashing: Wait until you are extremely close to an obstacle before activating your dash. Shattering an obstacle destroys it and earns bonus points, and your streak counter tracks how many you have shattered in a row.
  • Conserve Your Energy: Do not spam your dash as soon as it becomes available. Keep it ready for tight situations, especially when multiple high obstacles spawn back-to-back.
  • Stay Low: Double jumping is useful, but it keeps you in the air longer, making you vulnerable to fast-moving sentinel drones. Stay grounded as much as possible to react quickly.
